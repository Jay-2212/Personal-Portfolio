Privacy Policy

This page describes privacy-related behavior on the Jay Bharti portfolio site.

Analytics

The homepage loads Google Analytics with measurement ID G-6ZJ3F6T4CE to measure visits and page use. Google may process information through that service. Read Google's Privacy Policy to learn how Google handles information.

Theme preference

The homepage saves your dark or light theme choice in your browser's local storage under the key theme, so it can remember that setting when you return.

Contact and external links

The portfolio has no contact form. It provides an email link and links to external services. If you follow an external link, that service handles your visit under its own privacy practices.
